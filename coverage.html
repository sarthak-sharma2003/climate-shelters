      How this was worked out

      Enough to check it or redo it.

      Method and limits

      Shelters are the buildings on the county's cold-weather card, with hours from county and library web pages. Only cold is confirmed for any of them.

      Drive areas come from OpenStreetMap roads through Valhalla, an open-source router, with no traffic or winter conditions. Population is the county's 2024 municipal census by enumeration area, from the county's open data.

      A census area counts as within 10 minutes if its centre point is inside a drive area. Rural census areas are large, so the rural share is rough. It measures driving, so it understates the gap for people without a vehicle.

      Bus times come from Strathcona County Transit's schedule feed, valid to 12 December 2026.
