        Overnight shelters in Edmonton

        The county's cold-weather card lists no overnight shelter in Strathcona County. It sends people to these Edmonton shelters on regular county bus routes.

          Who to call

          • 911

            Someone is in serious distress, or it is an emergency.

          • 211

            Information and referral to community, social and health services. Open 24 hours, every day.

          • 780-464-4044

            Family and Community Services, 2nd floor, 401 Festival Lane. Monday to Friday during business hours. Emergency food and clothing, hygiene needs, a public phone, housing options and help getting to Edmonton shelters.

          • 780-467-7741

            RCMP and Enforcement Services non-emergency line, 24 hours. Call if you are worried about someone outside in the cold.

          Student prototype for a University of Alberta course project. It is not run or approved by Strathcona County.

          Warm places, shelters and phone numbers come from the county's cold-weather card (updated Oct. 2026). Hours come from county facility pages and the library's page, checked . On holidays with no published hours, places are listed under "Call first". Distances are straight-line, not travel distance.

          Your location is used on this device to sort the list. To draw the way to the closest open place, your location and that place are sent to a public OpenStreetMap routing service (routing.openstreetmap.de). This page saves nothing. The map loads from outside services (OpenStreetMap, unpkg). They can see that your browser asked for them, and the map service can see roughly which area the map is showing.
