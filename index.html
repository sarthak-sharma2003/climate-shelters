            Sources and privacy

            Places, shelters and phone numbers: the county's cold-weather card (Oct. 2026). Hours: county facility pages and the library's page, checked . A place is only shown as a shelter from something the county has confirmed. Holidays with no published hours show as "Call first". Distances are straight-line. Conditions: Environment Canada (alerts, air quality) and Open-Meteo (temperature), for Sherwood Park.

            Your location stays on this device, with two exceptions: the route is drawn by routing.openstreetmap.de, and a typed address is looked up by nominatim.openstreetmap.org. Map images come from OpenStreetMap, which can see roughly which area the map shows. Your last location and route are kept on this device only, so the way still shows with no signal: until the tab closes, or for 12 hours if you added this page to your home screen. "Clear" removes them.
